[t("hello world!"),m(10,10,r),s(5),b(l),k(ctrl,p),k(uni('a')),k(ctrl,r)]

The example above becomes something like this:

[
	Text("hello world!"),
	MoveMouse(10, 10, Relative),
	Scroll(5, Vertical),
	Button(Left, Click),
	Key(Control, Press),
	Key(Unicode('a'), Click),
	Key(Control, Release),
]

Vertical is the default for Scroll. Click is the default direction for Button and Key.
